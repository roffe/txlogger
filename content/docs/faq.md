## txlogger won't start: missing VCRUNTIME140.dll

Install the [Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 Runtime](https://aka.ms/vc14/vc_redist.x64.exe).

## txlogger won't start: OpenGL errors

Your system lacks proper OpenGL support. Download [Mesa3D for Windows](https://downloads.fdossena.com/geth.php?r=mesa64-latest) and unzip it into the txlogger folder.
