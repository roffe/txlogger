## Installation

1. Download the [installer](https://github.com/roffe/txlogger/releases/latest/download/txlogger_setup.zip) or the [standalone zip](https://github.com/roffe/txlogger/releases/latest/download/txlogger.zip) from the [downloads section](/#downloads).
2. Run the installer, or unzip the standalone version anywhere you like.
3. Connect your CAN adapter and start logging.

Requires Windows 10 or newer.
